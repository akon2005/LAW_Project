**Documents** lists the 25 most recent records of the bundled dataset, newest
first, with each record's court, year and area, plus a verification badge
("Verified" or "Synthetic").

**Preview** opens the same reading view used by search results. The list is
independent of the current search, so it is a convenient way to look around
without running a query.

Note that this tab browses the bundled dataset that ships with the frontend,
whereas the Research tab searches the indexed ChromaDB corpus. They are different
sources; the badge tells you which kind of record you are looking at.
