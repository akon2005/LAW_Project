## Where the controls are

**Settings** tab, section **Appearance & personalization**.

## Interface mode

Three options, chosen as a radio group (arrow keys move between them):

* **Light** — a clean, bright workspace.
* **Dark** — easy on the eyes in low light.
* **System default** — follows your device appearance.

## Colour palette

A gallery of palettes in two groups, "Neutral & professional" and "Colourful".
Selecting one restyles the interface through the theme tokens, so the courthouse
artwork, badges and meters follow the palette too.

## Custom palette

You can define your own palette from three colours — main background, accent
colour and primary highlight — and save or reset it.

## Independent reader theme

The reading view has its own theme (light / sepia / dark) and its own text-size,
spacing, width and typeface preferences. Changing your interface mode does not
change your reader theme, and reader preferences persist per browser under
`vidhiveda.reader`.

If a control you expect is missing here, it is not implemented in this build —
report it through the support assistant instead of assuming it exists.
